# Goal

Folio is a lightweight app for working with a local folder of Markdown notes. The folder is your library; the app is how you read and write it.

## What the final app does

**Open your notes.** Point Folio at the folder where your notes live. That folder is everything, and it stays yours.

**Write notes.** Open a page and type. Notes are plain text with lightweight formatting. Nothing to import, export, or convert.

**Link notes.** While writing you can link to other pages. Links are part of the writing. Clicking a link opens the page; linking to a page that does not exist yet creates a blank one.

**Backlinks.** Every page lists the pages that link to it, so related notes collect around a page without manual index maintenance.

**Daily notes.** One action opens today's page, wherever today lives. Yesterday's note is still there; tomorrow's does not exist until you need it.

**Search.** Search the text of every note in the vault from one field.

**Auto-save.** Everything you write is saved as you write it. Closing the window or losing power costs you nothing.

**Attach files.** Drag any file into a note; it is stored with your notes and becomes a link in the text.

**Works offline.** No connection, no account, no server, and nothing depends on a company staying in business.

**Your notes are files.** You can open, rename, move, or back up every note with any tool that reads Markdown. If the app disappears, your notes remain.

## Guardrail

One feature set, always: open folder, write notes, link, backlinks, daily notes, search, auto-save, offline. Do not add more.
