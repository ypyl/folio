# Links and backlinks

The right panel's Links section answers two questions about the open page: which
pages point at it, and which pages and files it points at.

> Demo guide. #Welcome has the table of contents.

## Backlinks

Every page that references this one appears under Links with an `in` badge.
#Welcome references this page from its table of contents, so Welcome is one of
them. A word reference and a bracketed reference count the same.

## Forward links

Scroll the same section and you find the pages this one references (#Welcome and
#References), plus the files and boards it names. The rows open the thing they
name.

## A page with no backlinks

A page nobody references has an empty backlinks list. The demo's Standalone note
is left out of every other page so it stays that way: open it from Files and look
at Links.

## Dates are links too

A reference whose name is a date opens that journal day, even when the day has no
note. #Journal shows this in practice.
